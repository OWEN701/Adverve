Questions Founders Ask

How quickly will our outreach be live?

Usually about a week from kickoff to the first campaign going out. New sending inboxes are warmed up first, so volume ramps up over the following weeks rather than starting at full speed.

Who owns the domains, inboxes and data?

You do. Everything is set up in your own accounts — sending domains, inboxes, lists, sequences and CRM workflows. If we stop working together, you keep all of it.

What does it cost?

A full outreach system build starts from £2,500 as a one-off. Ongoing campaign management starts from £1,500 a month, and a standalone list-building and enrichment project starts from £750. Exact pricing depends on volume and channels, and we confirm it on the call.

Will cold email hurt our main domain?

No. Campaigns send from separate secondary domains with SPF, DKIM and DMARC set up and inboxes warmed before anything goes out, so your main domain’s reputation stays protected.

What do you need from us?

A clear offer, a short kickoff on who your ideal customer is, and access to your CRM — or we set one up for you in GoHighLevel. We handle the lists, copy, tooling and sending.

Why not hire an SDR or do it in-house?

An SDR still needs the data, tooling, deliverability setup and CRM behind them, and takes months to ramp. We build that system and run it for less than the cost of a hire — and if you do bring outreach in-house later, the whole system is already yours.

Do you work with companies outside the UK?

Yes. We work with B2B companies across the UK and North America.
