Privacy Policy

Last updated: 11 November 2026

This policy explains what personal data Adverve collects through www.adverve.io, why, and what your rights are. Adverve is run by Owen Cawston in the United Kingdom, who is the data controller for this website. Questions or requests: owen@adverve.io.

Who processes it for us

  • Google (Google Analytics 4) — website analytics, only after consent.
  • Supabase — stores chat assistant conversations.
  • Anthropic — generates the chat assistant’s replies from the messages you send.
  • Lemcal — call booking.
  • Netlify — website hosting.
  • Microsoft (Outlook) — email.

Some of these providers process data outside the UK, including in the United States. Where they do, transfers rely on the UK’s adequacy regulations (such as the UK–US data bridge) or standard contractual clauses.

Cookies

Google Analytics cookies (_ga, _ga_*) are only set if you click “Accept” in the cookie banner. The site also stores small preferences in your browser — your cookie choice and whether you have already seen the intro animation — which are not used for tracking. To change your cookie choice, clear this site’s data in your browser and the banner will appear again.

How long we keep it

Chat conversations and enquiry emails are kept for up to 24 months after your last contact, unless you become a client, in which case they are kept for as long as needed to provide the service and meet legal obligations. Analytics data is kept for 14 months.

Your rights

Under UK data protection law you can ask to access, correct or delete your data, object to or restrict how it is used, withdraw consent at any time, and ask for a copy of data you provided. Email owen@adverve.io and we will respond within one month. You can also complain to the Information Commissioner’s Office at ico.org.uk.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted here with a new date.
